The recommendation system on Spotify in of itself presents an interesting form of music suggestion, given that users aren't expected, or even allowed, to control the direction of their recommendations through exact terms, aiming to provide a laid-back experience by learning from the user's taste profile and guessing based on multiple parameters what to show them. Spotify's algorithm uses listening history to determine which categories of music are in closer alignment to the user's preferences, tracking down what music they enjoy through their saved "liked songs" collection, how often they repeat certain songs during playback, and how long they spend listening to a new track before moving onto the next(eg. listening to 30s of a new track counts it as a positive in determining future recommendations). In other words, Spotify uses machine learning to transform a track's energy and scope into a listable set of variables. On top of this process, metadata is used as additional context in recommending music, such as the track's genre, to filter out unrelated results from potential recommendations for the user. Collaborative filtering is the final influence layer on what songs get recommended, meaning any given track the user likes which belongs to an artist will lead to recommendations either from the same artist, or other artists which fans of the same song listen to afterwards. In a sense, because you lean towards a specific style, you'll be recommended music that others with similar behaviors check out after the fact. To summarize, the recommendation model on Spotify is among the most advanced on the music provider market, and an industry staple. From it we understand an approach that leans away from manual categorization of music, instead opting for reinforced learning through individual behavioral patterns in users through listening history, as well as group behavioral patterns through collaborative filtering, using metadata only to narrow down potential selections. Alongside these, the user is given control through AI prompt tools to dictate the style and energy of the music they want to listen to, although this technology is still in its initial stages of development.
