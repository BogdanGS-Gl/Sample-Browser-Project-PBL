## Solution

New idea: You can check out tags describing the current song and apply them to filter the rest of recommendations. You can stack multiple tags to filter recommendations(eg. if you have "drums" and "indie", you get "indie + drums", "drums" and "indie" music.)

Then if you like your current queue you can save it to menu and check it out later.

This way users can make their own niche discovery channels and find music they enjoy more as they listen to it.
